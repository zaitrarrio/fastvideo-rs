## Why not Burn, Candle, cuda-oxide, Tile-IR or raw PTX

Asked 2026-11-25. Candle's non-cuDNN conv is im2col + cuBLAS with no conv3d
(ADR-0001 retired it from the tree); Burn's CubeCL implicit GEMM is JIT
through NVRTC per shape inside its own runtime, against ADR-0024's AOT and
single-runtime rules. `cuda-oxide` (NVlabs, Rust → PTX through a nightly
rustc backend; `docker/oxide.Dockerfile`) and Tile-IR (`cutile-rs` +
`tileiras`, the embedded NVFP4 GEMM) are both in the tree and both measured
well under the vendor libraries (`docs/perf/datacenter-kernel-plan.md` §3.1:
`gemm_sol` 58 % of cuBLASLt SoL; Tile-IR NVFP4 lost to cuBLASLt); the plan
keeps them off the critical path, and a conv would be a new kernel in either
with the same tuning work as CUDA C and a second toolchain to maintain. "Raw
PTX" is what the kernels already do where it matters: `attn_dc.cu` carries
hand-written PTX (TMA, mbarrier, wgmma, tcgen05) inside CUDA C compiled by
nvcc to cubins per SM; a conv written entirely in PTX gains nothing over
inline `asm` for the `mma.sync` / `ldmatrix` / `cp.async` pieces and loses
the compiler for the rest. So the candidates are CUDA C in `rt/kernels/`
(the AOT table, the existing `mma.sync` tiles) and CUTLASS through a host
shim, with explicit unfold + cuBLASLt as the zero-kernel baseline.

## WORK ORDER 3b-ii: the warp-specialised mainloop

Owner decision 2026-11-25 (FVID-2026-11-25-conv-igemm-ws-wo, "2"): after
the three capped tuning rounds found no faster kernel, one more work
order on the kernel's mainloop before the margin decision.

### Objective
The implicit-GEMM conv reaches the owner's proposed margin, ≤ 1.10×
cuDNN, on the shapes that carry the decode wall time (wan22-1024-s1,
wan22-512-s2, ltx-1024, ltx-up-4096 in `conv_routes`; the LTX-2.5
1080p20s decode in `convgate`), with a mainloop in which the warps that
compute never issue a global load.

### What the three rounds established (docs/perf/2026-11-25-conv-routes.md)
- The kernel with no global loads at all runs at 0.92-0.98× cuDNN on
  these shapes (`probe_noload`): the mma / ldmatrix / barrier structure
  at two CTAs per SM is cuDNN's speed. cuDNN's kernels on sm_120 hide
  their loads inside the same budget; this kernel pays 35-40 points for
  them: 17-20 for the gather's address work (`probe_dense` vs the real
  kernel), 20-23 for the plain cp.async traffic (`probe_noload` vs
  `probe_dense`).
- Two CTAs per SM are required (every one-CTA variant lost 20-50 %), and
  compute capability 12.0's 128 KB of shared memory per SM caps a
  two-CTA kernel at 63 KB: the 48 KB three-stage ring of the base kernel
  is the deepest ring available. A deeper ring is not an option on this
  GPU.
- Registers: the base kernel (8 warps of 64×32) uses 114 at two CTAs per
  SM; the 4-warp 64×64 variant (`w64`) uses 220 at the same speed.

So the load cost is not latency a deeper ring would hide; it is issue
time and address arithmetic in the warps that should be issuing mma. The
fix is to move the loads out of those warps.

### Design (pinned)
- **CTA = 4 consumer warps + 1 producer warp (160 threads), 128×128×32
  tile, 64×64 warp tiles, 3-stage 48 KB ring, two CTAs per SM**
  (`__launch_bounds__(160, 2)`: 204 registers available per thread; the
  consumer needs the `w64` variant's 220 minus the loader's registers,
  to be read from ptxas; a spill is a STOP). The `w64` variant proved
  the 4-warp consumer side runs at the base kernel's speed.
- **Producer warp**: for stage `s` of k-tile `kt` it waits on the
  stage's `empty` barrier, issues the A tile (the gather: 128 rows × 32
  k = 512 chunks of 16 B, 16 per lane) and the B tile (weights, 512
  chunks, 16 per lane) with `cp.async.cg.shared.global` + zfill, commits
  the group, then `cp.async.wait_group(STAGES-2)` and arrives on the
  stage's `full` barrier. Named barriers (`bar.arrive` / `bar.sync` with
  ids 1..2·STAGES, thread count 160), sm_80+ (no mbarrier, no sm_90
  dependency: the kernel must run on every SM the binary carries from
  sm_80 up; sm_75 keeps the 3c route).
- **Consumer warps**: `bar.sync full[s]`, the k-tile's fragments and 64
  mma per warp as today (two-deep fragment ring across the two k16
  chunks), `bar.arrive empty[s]`. No `__syncthreads` in the mainloop.
- **The gather's arithmetic moves with it**, and is cheaper there: the
  producer keeps per-lane (d, h, w) origins of its 16 rows in registers
  (decoded once per CTA), so per k-tile a tap is three adds and three
  compares per row; the channel chunk is a loop over 8-channel groups
  with one wrap. The "tap decoded once per k-tile" hoisting of e78a557
  stays.
- **Epilogue** unchanged in this WO (bf16 `out16` for the LTX path, f32
  planes for split-K); a timing probe `probe_noepi` (no stores) is added
  so the record says what the epilogue costs before anything is spent on
  it.
- **Entry points**: `conv_igemm_bf16_ws` beside the base kernel (the
  variant list grows by `ws` and `probe_noepi`; `bk64`, `s4`, `s6` go,
  their numbers stay in the record). The default variant flips to `ws`
  only when the acceptance below is met; until then `w32` stays.
- Split-K rule unchanged (CTAs counted with the 128×128 tile).

### Acceptance criteria (executable)
- [ ] `conv_routes` on the PRO 6000: `igemm_ws` ≤ 1.10× cuDNN bf16 on
      wan22-1024-s1, wan22-512-s2, ltx-1024, ltx-up-4096, and no shape
      slower than `w32` by more than 5 %; every correctness check as
      today (rel_l2 ≤ 5e-3 vs host, rel_l1 ≤ 2e-3 vs cuDNN bf16).
- [ ] `convgate` (LTX-2.5 1080p20s fast decode, cuDNN NHWC vs igemm):
      wall time ≤ 1.10× (today 1.42×), PSNR / LPIPS at today's level
      (55.8 dB, 1.4e-4).
- [ ] ptxas (`-arch=sm_120 -Xptxas -v` on the generated `kernels.cu`):
      `conv_igemm_bf16_ws` ≤ 204 registers, 0 spill bytes, 49152 bytes
      static smem; the same on sm_80, sm_86, sm_89, sm_90, sm_100.
- [ ] Determinism unchanged (no atomics, fixed split); `det-short` on the
      kernels tier.
- [ ] `task check`, the kernel-source pin tests, `conv_routes` records
      committed under `artifacts/runpod/rtx6000/`, the perf record
      extended with a "WO-3b-ii" section.

### Kill criterion (STOP and report, do not keep tuning)
- If after the kernel is correct and at two CTAs per SM with no spills
  the four carrying shapes are still above 1.15× cuDNN in `conv_routes`,
  stop: record the numbers, do not run `convgate`, and put the margin
  decision to the owner with the three options of 2026-11-25 (accept
  the slowdown on the static image only; park step 3; a datacentre-only
  wgmma / tcgen05 kernel as a later WP-G).

### Non-goals
- Deeper rings or larger CTA tiles (closed by the rounds). sm_90a wgmma
  / sm_100a tcgen05 or TMA variants (WP-G). The epilogue (measured by the
  probe, not changed). Channels-last residency between convs. 3c, 3d.

### Decision points — STOP and ask
- A spill at `__launch_bounds__(160, 2)` that costs more than 5 % against
  the 4-warp consumer at one CTA per SM (then the design question is
  two producer warps and 64×32 consumer tiles, 288 threads).
- The kill criterion above.
- GPU spend: the kernels runs cost about $0.3-0.7 each (900 s cap);
  the `convgate` gate about $1.5. The Runpod balance is $8.60 against
  the $8 floor (CLAUDE.md), so the gate and more than one kernels run
  need a top-up of about $10 first.

### Verification
- The `conv_routes` tables (ws beside w32 and the probes) and the
  `convgate` table pasted into the perf record; ptxas lines pasted into
  the PR. UNVERIFIED until run: every number above the "established"
  list.

### Decision log entry (proposed)
FVID · <date> · FVID-<date>-conv-igemm-ws: the warp-specialised mainloop
is / is not the served kernel; `conv_routes` <ratios>, `convgate`
<ratio>; the margin decision <accepted / parked>. ADR-0024 step 3.

## WORK ORDER 3c: the small kernels and the GEMM-shaped convs

### Objective
Every remaining cuDNN conv call site has an in-tree path: the 1-D audio
convs (LTX vocoder, H3 audio VAE), transposed 1-D convs, depthwise /
grouped convs (anti-alias filters, spark), the stride-2 encoder convs if
3b's kernel lacks them, ESRGAN's forced-FMA f32 convs, and the convs that
are GEMMs (patch embeds with k = s). Non-served sites (Hunyuan 1.5,
Kandinsky 5, MMAudio, Sana, Flux2, LPIPS) follow the same paths so the
library can go.

### Acceptance criteria (executable)
- [ ] `tensor.rs`: a conv with k = s, pad 0 (Wan `patch_embed`, H3
      vision k = s = [2,16,16], CLIP / Synchformer patch embeds) runs as
      space-to-depth (one permute kernel) + the existing GEMM; bit-level
      parity with the host reference at f32 tolerance (gpucheck rows
      `patch_conv_{wan,h3}`).
- [ ] `rt/kernels/conv_direct.cu`: a direct N-D convolution (1-D / 2-D /
      3-D, groups incl. depthwise, dilation, stride, pad), f32 FMA,
      one output element per thread with the weights of one output
      channel in shared memory; and its transposed form (gather per
      output sample: convT1d for the vocoders, `determinism_conv_transpose1d`
      stays byte-stable across runs). Tolerance vs the host twins
      (`ops::host::conv1d`, `conv_transpose1d`, `conv2d`) at today's
      `cmp` thresholds in the `gemm` group.
- [ ] Dense conv1d with C ≥ 256 (vocoder resblocks, k 3/7/11 dilated)
      routes to 3b's kernel with D = H = 1 (dilation is a kernel param);
      the depthwise 12-tap anti-alias and the convT go to `conv_direct`.
- [ ] ESRGAN: `cudnn_conv_fma` → `conv_direct` f32; the `esrgan_*`
      device-vs-host rows keep passing at their current tolerances
      (`esrgan_pack_*` byte-exact is downstream of the conv and
      unchanged); `esrgan_480x272x81_x4` bench reported (speed is not
      gated: CLI only).
- [ ] LPIPS (gpucheck scoring) on `conv_direct` or 3b's kernel; its
      scores on the gold compare clips move by < 1e-3 (recorded).
- [ ] Audio gates: `h3arms` / `ltxfps` cells that produce audio: the
      waveform vs the cuDNN-built arm, SNR ≥ 60 dB (f32 FMA both sides,
      different sum order).
- [ ] `task check` green; every `cudnn_*` symbol outside `conv.rs`,
      `cudnn_sdpa.rs` and gpucheck's reference group is gone.

### Context
- Audio sizes: LTX vocoder hidden 1536, L up to about 320 k samples per
  10 s; H3 audio decoder_dim 1024, B = 2. These are memory-bound; a
  direct kernel at full bandwidth is within 2× of cuDNN's and the
  decode is a small share of the clip. Measure, do not tune beyond that.
- `h3/vdn.cu` shows the house style for a depthwise conv kernel.

### Non-goals
- Performance work on audio; new fusions; Hunyuan / Kandinsky / MMAudio
  quality gates beyond the per-conv tolerance (no presets serve them).

### Decision points — STOP and ask
- Any audio SNR under 60 dB; any ESRGAN row outside tolerance.

### Verification
- gpucheck rows and the two audio compares with pasted output.

### Decision log entry (proposed)

f32 convs on `conv_direct`, k = s convs on the GEMM; cuDNN called from no
served or CLI path. ADR-0024 step 3.
