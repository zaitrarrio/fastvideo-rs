## 1. Director + local upsampler

### Session on FastH3 Trim (480p, no image)

`configure`: `upsample: true`, a **14 896-character premise** (a series bible, 3 503 tokens by the LLM's
`/tokenize`), a style, **3 entities** (2 characters, 1 location), seed 1013, 480p; then 3 directions:
v2 `replan: true` after chunk 1, v3 `replan: false` after chunk 2, v4 `replan: true` after chunk 3; stop after 5
chunks. `artifacts/…/director-trim/` (summary, every event, the server log, a 21 s clip, a frame sheet).

| chunk | prompt v | `segment_expanded` latency | prompt_length / limit | fallback | chunk generation |
|---:|---:|---:|---|---|---:|
| 0 | 1 | 8 046 ms | 650 / 1024 tokens | no | 25.8 s |
| 1 | 1 | 21 017 ms | 700 / 1024 tokens | no | 25.6 s |
| 2 | 2 | **30 001 ms** | 26 / 1024 tokens | **yes**: `upsampler LLM timed out after 30000 ms` | 23.3 s |
| 3 | 4 | 9 396 ms | 791 / 1024 tokens | no | 25.6 s |
| 4 | 4 | 27 982 ms | 1004 / 1024 tokens | no | 26.3 s |

- **H3 accepted every Context-IR prompt**: all within 1024 tokens by H3's own tokenizer (`prompt_unit: "tokens"`,
  the server logged `prompt limits are in tokens`), no refusal, no error. The opening segment introduced no new entity.
- **Directions**: v2 (replan) was applied at chunk 2 but its expansion was the fallback (the raw direction, 26
  tokens); v3 (append) was superseded by v4 (replan) before its chunk came up (fal's replan semantics: the deck is
  rebuilt), so it never played; v4 was applied at chunk 3 (9.4 s after it was sent).
- **Timing**: time to first frame 34.7 s (8.0 s opening expansion + 25.8 s chunk 0). A 5.17 s chunk takes 23-26 s
  (`generation_over_playback` 4.9), so **3 of the 5 chunks were `deadline_missed`** (behaviour
  `freeze_video_and_silence_audio_until_ready`). The client received 2 820 frames at 24.08 fps, max frame gap
  144 ms (frozen frames keep flowing), 7 587 Opus packets; the mp4 is 117.5 s of which 25.8 s are new content.
- **The sidecar while the GPU generates** (llama-server's own timings): decode **32-38 tok/s** during Trim's denoise
  vs **76-88 tok/s** with the GPU otherwise idle (and 97 tok/s in the sidecar's own bench); prefill stays fast
  (premise cached: 22-932 new tokens per request, 0.08-0.13 s). Director segments emit 600-1 050 tokens of JSON, so a
  segment takes 9-28 s next to a running chunk; the one that crossed `timeout_ms` = 30 000 fell back.
- **VRAM peak** (nvidia-smi, whole GPU, 1 s samples): **51.4 GB** during the session (sidecar 11.1 GB + fv-serve);
  52.7 GB over the whole run. Engine `peak_memory_mb` 29.0 GiB (480p) / 35.6 GiB (768p) — see the offload note below.
- Frames: `director-trim/sheet.jpg`. The hall, the frogs, the incense bowl and the arched windows match the bible;
  "Master Bramble" was rendered as an old man in some frames. Chunks 0-3 look alike (a text-to-video model has no
  last-frame anchor: continuity is a crossfade).

### `image_url` on Trim: refused only after the work was done (fixed)

`configure` with `image_url` on the Trim app was **accepted** (`configured`, `has_initial_image`), the opening was
upsampled (7.96 s of LLM work with the image), and then chunk 0 failed:
`{"type":"error","code":"generation_failed","error":"model `fasth3-trim-8step` cannot build I2V clips"}` and the
session closed (`director-trim-image-refused/`). Trim serves text-to-video only (caps.rs `h3_caps`). **Fix:** the
director's `Limits` now carry `image_conditioning` (the model supports I2V; causal models already refused), and
`configure` refuses `image_url`, `end_image_url` and script end images, and `prompt` end images, with
`invalid_initial_image` / `invalid_initial_script` / `prompt_rejected(invalid_image)` ("this model is text-to-video
only"), before any LLM call or chunk (test `director::control::tests::t2v_only_clip_model_refuses_images`).

Stopped after 2 chunks to stay within budget (see below).

`output` (default `cloudflare`, `FV_CF_STREAM_NAME_PREFIX=fv-test-`), WHEP read-back, and delete the `fv-test-` input.

## 3. FastH3 Trim: bf16 vs MXFP8 (same pod, same request)

`POST /fv/v1/jobs` `{model: fasth3-trim-8step, seed: 1024, num_frames: 124, the matrix-style talking-head prompt
with <d> dialogue}`, run twice per point after the server's own warm-up; the second run is the table (the first
agreed within 0.1 s of denoise). Default = the served recipe (MXFP8 linears in blocks 2-38 on sm_120); bf16 =
`FASTVIDEO_H3_QUANT=off` on a restarted server, everything else equal. Per-step times from fv-serve's
`h3 step i/8` log lines; `bench/` has every job object.

| 124 frames (5.2 s), warm | MXFP8 (default) | bf16 (`=off`) | MXFP8 speed-up |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 832x480: s/step | **1.6** (1.6 × 8) | 2.1 (2.1-2.2) | 1.32x |
| 832x480: denoise | **13.00 s** | 17.12 s | 1.32x |
| 832x480: end to end (job wall, text cached) | **17.1 s** | 22.3 s | 1.30x |
| 832x480: video decode / audio decode | 3.41 / 0.24 s | 3.93 / 0.26 s | |
| 1344x768: s/step | **4.7** | 6.0 | 1.27x |
| 1344x768: denoise | **37.95 s** | 48.28 s | 1.27x |
| 1344x768: end to end | **45.0 s** | 55.8 s | 1.24x |
| engine `peak_memory_mb` 480p / 768p | 29 686 / 36 480 MB | 29 686 / 36 480 MB | |
| whole-GPU peak (incl. the 11 GB sidecar) | 52.4 GB | 49.7 GB | |

**Caveat — the DiT was streamed in both arms.** With the sidecar resident the engine's resident plan (64.6 GiB, FP8
text encoder included) did not fit the 60.6 GiB left, so `h3 dit offload: auto -> streamed`: every run copied the
blocks from host memory (163 GiB H2D per 480p run with MXFP8, 266 GiB with bf16) at 57.8 GB/s, **100% hidden behind
compute** (exposed 0.00 s). The comparison is therefore compute against compute, as resident runs would be; the
offload also explains why `peak_memory_mb` is the same for both. It was not repeated without the sidecar.

**Recommendation: keep MXFP8 as the sm_120 default for Trim; do not change it.** On one pod, same request, MXFP8
is 1.27-1.32x faster at both 480p and 768p. The earlier note (bf16 2.14-2.22 s vs MXFP8 2.48-2.54 s at 480p,
docs/ports/h3-trim.md §6.2) compared two different pods (Workstation Edition); on this Server Edition pod both arms
are faster than either number and the order is reversed. **FastH3 V2: no change proposed.** V2 was not re-measured
(the served V2 recipe is `fasth3-8step-dense`, not the VSA run of §6.2, and its tree is 81 GB from R2); nothing here
suggests its default is wrong. If someone wants to re-check, run both arms once more on a Workstation Edition pod
without the sidecar.

## Bugs found

1. **Director accepts `image_url` on a text-to-video-only model** (Trim), runs the LLM, then fails chunk 0.
   **Fixed** (above), with a unit test.
2. **Upsampler token limits fall back to characters on Trim.** The default H3 tokenizer path is
   `<model weights>/tokenizer/tokenizer.json`; the Trim tree has no tokenizer (the engine reads it from `h3-base`, the
   catalog's `text_weights`), so with the shipped `runpod-h3-trim.toml` the limit would be in characters (3200),
   not 1024 tokens. This run set `[upsampler.tokenizers] h3` explicitly. **Fixed** in `fastvideo-serve/src/tokens.rs`:
   for an H3 model the text tree is the entry's `text_weights`, and for Trim (tree `fasth3-trim-*` or recipe
   `h3-trim` / `trim-8step`) the `h3-base` tree beside it (test `tokens::tests::trim_counts_with_the_h3_base_tokenizer`).
3. **Director `x_fv_output` over WHIP carries no audio** (not fixed). Repro: an H3 director session (audio on:
   `director session starting … audio=true`) with `configure.x_fv_output = {"kind": "whip", "url":
   "http://127.0.0.1:8889/<path>/whip", "target": "mediamtx"}` and `[outputs] allow_private`; MediaMTX logs
   `is publishing to path '<path>', 1 track (H264)`; the main WebRTC peer gets Opus (7 587 packets in the Trim
   session). RTMP/SRT sinks of native streams do carry audio. The `output_copies` test asserts "H.264 + audio
   copied", so check what the in-process WHIP endpoint accepts versus MediaMTX (the copy's offer may omit the audio
   m-line, or `OutputMedia.audio_channels` may be `None` for clip sessions).
4. **The local sidecar next to H3 needs different defaults** (not changed; proposals):
   - `FV_UPSAMPLER_TIMEOUT_MS=30000` is too short while a chunk denoises: decode drops to 32-38 tok/s and segments emit
     up to ~1 050 tokens (≈ 30 s). Raise it to 60 000 for co-located sidecars, or cap `max_tokens` for the segment.
   - `deck_size` 6 makes the director plan 6 LLM segments ahead (17 calls for 3 played segments in the turbo session);
     next to a video model a deck of 2 would cut contention.
   - The 11 GB sidecar pushes H3 out of its resident plan on 96 GB: the Trim DiT is streamed (free here: hidden), but
     the **H3 i2v encoder is streamed from the network volume per image-conditioned build**, making anchored
     director chunks on turbo/max take minutes. docs/serve/upsampler-local.md §4 says "on a 96 GB RTX PRO 6000 … there
     is room beside every family"; for anchored H3 directors it is not. Options: a smaller sidecar
     (`FV_UPSAMPLER_CTX=32768 FV_UPSAMPLER_PARALLEL=1`, ≈ 8.5 GB), the i2v encoder from local disk instead of the
     volume, or the sidecar on another GPU.
5. Harness (fixed): `pod.sh keyrun` exports `FV_KEY` (a `K=V` prefix reaches only the first command); `pod.sh get`
   / the sidecar's `GET /file/…` download results; `pod.sh bundle <tgz>`; `FV_E2E_DISK_GB`; `FV_E2E_NO_SECRETS`.
