# Open-weight video generation landscape (non-Wan), as of 2026-10-21

Scope: every serious open-weight video generation model released or materially updated in 2025–2026 other than Alibaba's Wan family (Wan-derived models are noted). All release dates are as given by the cited primary source (GitHub/HF/arXiv) where available; where only secondary sources exist that is stated. Licence text is quoted verbatim from the LICENSE files where the researcher could fetch them.

Source-quality note: Artificial Analysis (AA) leaderboard numbers are from the live AA pages fetched 2026-10-21; VBench numbers are from a third-party mirror (benchmarklist.com, last updated 2026-10-03) because the official VBench HF Space would not render; no VBench-2.0 table could be retrieved (see Gaps).

## Key question 1: Which open-weight models are closest to the closed frontier in quality right now, and which have the most permissive licences for commercial derivatives?

### Takeaway
MiniMax-H3 (33B, weights 2026-08-03) is the only open-weight model inside the closed frontier band on AA (Elo 1158 T2V, 15 points behind #1 Wan 3.0 and ahead of Veo 3.1, Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.0), but its licence excludes the USA, EU, UK and Korea and caps commercial revenue at $20M without written authorisation; the next open tier (MAGI-2 Preview 1044–1046, LTX-2.5 ~950–984) is 110–200 Elo lower but MAGI-2 is Apache-2.0 and LTX-2.5 is free under $10M revenue, while the fully permissive (MIT/Apache) models that also have training code are Kandinsky 6.0/5.0 (MIT), MOVA (Apache-2.0), Open-Sora 2.0 (Apache-2.0) and Sana-Video 2.0 (licence not stated), none of which are on the AA board.

## Key question 3: Which models are architecturally most similar to Wan (so Wan know-how, kernels and distillation recipes transfer) versus very different (single-stream, audio-visual, autoregressive)?

### Takeaway
Direct Wan derivatives (weights initialised from Wan2.2) are Ovi (video branch from Wan2.2), LingBot-World 1.x (Wan2.2 i2v-A14B) and LingBot-World 2.0 (Wan2.2, causal); Wan-adjacent dense cross-attention DiTs with T5-class encoders are LongCat-Video (13.6B, UMT5-XXL), Kandinsky 5.0 (cross-attention DiT, Qwen2.5-VL+CLIP, HunyuanVideo VAE) and SkyReels-V3 (acknowledges Wan 2.1); while MiniMax-H3 (33B single-stream, Qwen3-VL encoder, f16t4 VAE, joint stereo audio), MAGI-2 (114B MoE single-stream unified text+video+audio sequence, Wan2.2 VAE), LTX-2.x (joint AV DiT, Gemma 4 encoder, 32×8 VAE), Kandinsky 6.0 (dual-stream CrossDiT AV), SANA-Video 2.0 (hybrid linear attention) and Cosmos 3 (mixture-of-transformers AR+diffusion) are structurally different.

### Cited Findings
- Ovi: "The video branch is 'initialized from the Wan2.2 repository'"; audio branch 5B "designed and pretrained from scratch"; 11B total (5B+5B+1B fusion per secondary); ComfyUI support "via ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper" — [character-ai/Ovi](https://github.com/character-ai/Ovi); [comfyui-wiki Ovi](https://comfyui-wiki.com/en/models/ovi)
- LingBot-World 1.x: "Built on Wan2.2. The scripts use the i2v-A14B task flag and a T5 text encoder"; Fast variant uses "KV-cached chunk-by-chunk causal inference" — [lingbot-world GitHub](https://github.com/robbyant/lingbot-world). LingBot-World 2.0: "Built upon Wan2.2", "causal pretraining, with a real-time variant distilled from the base model", KV-cached chunked inference; 14B/1.3B DiT weights share T5/VAE/tokenizer with Wan — [lingbot-world-v2](https://github.com/Robbyant/lingbot-world-v2)
- MAGI-2 Preview: "Video VAE is from Wan2.2-TI2V-5B"; text encoder Qwen3.5-27B; audio VAE stable-audio-open-1.0; MoE 114B/6B active; two-stage preview (512×896) + refiner (1088×1920); fixed 10 s — [sand-ai/MAGI-2-preview](https://huggingface.co/sand-ai/MAGI-2-preview). MiniMax blog: "dropped the Hailuo-02 architecture"; 2K via "In-context Regeneration" rather than a separate SR module; ~30% training-throughput gain from splitting understanding/generation workloads — [MiniMax H3 blog](https://www.minimax.io/blog/minimax-h3)

- LTX-2.5: DiT with custom Gemma 4 12B encoder (Google's stock Gemma 4 "is not a substitute"); DiffVAE (diffusion decoder) or conv VAE; audio VAE; spatial ×2 and temporal ×2 latent upscalers; 8-step distilled CFG=1 — [LTX-2 GitHub](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2); [LTX-2.5 card](https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5). LTX 2.3 VAE geometry: "128 latent channels, (8, 32, 32) temporal/spatial strides" — [Sana sana_video2.md](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana/blob/main/docs/sana_video2.md)
- Kandinsky 5.0: "Diffusion Transformer (DiT) backbone uses cross-attention to text embeddings"; Qwen2.5-VL + CLIP; "HunyuanVideo 3D VAE"; NABLA sparse attention for 10 s — [Kandinsky-5 GitHub](https://github.com/kandinskylab/Kandinsky-5). Kandinsky 6.0: "dual-stream CrossDiT: a pretrained video stream and a newly trained audio stream, linked by bidirectional cross-attention" — [paper page](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.05608)
- LongCat-Video: dense 13.6B; UMT5-XXL credited; 720p 30 fps; natively pretrained on video continuation; coarse-to-fine; block-sparse attention — [LongCat-Video HF](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-Video)
- SkyReels-V3: acknowledges "Wan 2.1, MultiTalk, xDiT, and diffusers"; 14B R2V/V2V and 19B A2V DiTs; multi-GPU via xDiT USP — [SkyReels-V3 GitHub](https://github.com/SkyworkAI/SkyReels-V3)
- SANA-Video 2.0: "75% gated bidirectional linear-attention layers, 25% dense softmax anchors", SwiGLU, "Wan 3D rotary position embeddings", Attention Residual in blocks of 8; LTX 2.3 VAE; Gemma 2 — [sana_video2.md](https://github.com/NVlabs/Sana/blob/main/docs/sana_video2.md)
- MOVA: "asymmetric dual-tower design that uses 'pre-trained video and audio towers' fused via bidirectional cross-attention"; MoE 32B/18B active; Wan2.2 acknowledged but backbone not stated — [OpenMOSS-Team/MOVA-720p](https://huggingface.co/OpenMOSS-Team/MOVA-720p); [MOVA GitHub](https://github.com/OpenMOSS/MOVA)
- Cosmos 3: "Mixture-of-Transformers (MoT) with two towers: an autoregressive transformer for discrete tokens (text) and a diffusion transformer for continuous generation (images, video, audio, actions)" — [nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Cosmos3-Nano)
- Step-Video-T2V: 30B, 48 layers, 3D full attention, flow matching, VAE 16×16 spatial / 8× temporal, two bilingual encoders, AdaLN-Single — [Step-Video-T2V](https://github.com/stepfun-ai/Step-Video-T2V)
- Mochi 1: AsymmDiT 48 layers, AsymmVAE 8×8×6 12 ch, T5-XXL — [genmoai/mochi](https://github.com/genmoai/mochi)
- daVinci-MagiHuman: "single-stream Transformer that processes text, video, and audio within a unified token sequence via self-attention only"; 15B, 40 layers (secondary) — [neurohive](https://neurohive.io/en/state-of-the-art/davinci-magihuman-open-15b-model-generates-a-5-second-lip-sync-video-in-2-seconds-on-a-single-h100/)
- Wan-like VAE geometry: H3 f16t4 (24 ch), HunyuanVideo 1.5 f16t4 (32 ch), Step-Video f16t8, LTX 2.3 f32t8 (128 ch), Mochi f8t6 (12 ch) — sources as above.

### Inferences
- Transfer of Wan kernels/recipes is near-total for Ovi and LingBot-World (same DiT, T5 and VAE; LingBot-World 2.0 even reuses Wan assets via `--assets_dir`), but LingBot-World 2.0 is non-commercial and Ovi lacks training code. - Distillation recipes (DMD/DMD2, Self-Forcing causal) have already been ported to H3 and LingBot-Video by FastVideo/lightx2v, indicating that Wan-era distillation know-how transfers across these architectures in practice.

- Primary architectural details for MAGI-2 (expert count, routing, whether it is bidirectional) and MOVA's video tower origin were not confirmed; the MAGI-2 tech report was not located.
- Kandinsky 6.0's video VAE and text encoder, and LongCat-Video's VAE, are not stated in the pages fetched.

- The open frontier is now being pushed by labs that monetise differently (Lightricks' revenue-tiered licence, Sand.ai's hosted MAGI-2 API, Sber, Meituan/Ant as non-video businesses, NVIDIA for hardware pull-through), so a team choosing a base should expect its future upgrades to come from these labs rather than from Alibaba/ByteDance/Kuaishou.

### Gaps

## Key question 5: Which models have the best size/quality tradeoff for serving on one 80–96 GB GPU?

### Takeaway
On a single 80–96 GB card the realistic candidates are LTX-2.5 (22B + Gemma 4 12B, fp8/int8/NVFP4 and 8-step distilled, ~66 GiB download), Kandinsky 6.0 Pro (29B, benchmarked on one H100/A100 at 88–134 s per distilled 5 s clip), HunyuanVideo 1.5 (8.3B, 14 GB minimum, 75 s on a 4090 step-distilled) and MiniMax-H3 (33B; 123.6 GB in full precision but 42.5 GB with int8 variants, 4–8-step LoRAs from lightx2v/FastVideo); MAGI-2 (8 Hopper GPUs, 307 GB) and LingBot-Video MoE (peak ~82 GiB on a 140 GiB card) do not fit comfortably, and Cosmos 3 Super (65B) is documented only on H100/GB200/H200 with BF16.

### Inferences
- Quality-per-GB ranking for one 80–96 GB GPU (combining AA Elo where available with footprint): (1) MiniMax-H3 int8 + 4/8-step LoRA (Elo 1158 base; ~42 GB) if the licence permits the deployment territory; (2) LTX-2.5 distilled fp8/NVFP4 (Elo ~950; fits with offload; cleanest commercial path under $10M); (3) Kandinsky 6.0 Pro distilled (no AA score; MIT; ~90–130 s per clip on H100); (4) HunyuanVideo 1.5 (8.3B, smallest, fastest, no audio, not EU-licensable). MAGI-2's 114B MoE needs a multi-GPU node despite 6B active parameters because the 228 GB preview transformer must be resident.
- The adoption pattern (ComfyUI day-0, lightx2v/FastVideo distills within 1–4 weeks, fal hosting) concentrated on H3 and LTX-2.5 in Aug–Sep 2026 and on Kandinsky 6.0 in Oct 2026; HunyuanVideo 1.5 remains the consumer-GPU default; LongCat, LingBot-Video, MOVA and Sana-Video have far thinner tooling.
